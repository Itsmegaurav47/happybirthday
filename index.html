📖 Our Fairy Tale

The story of a boy, a girl, and the miles between them that never mattered

📱
Chapter One

Once Upon a Swipe

About four years ago, in the little digital world of an app called Tantan, a boy's thumb paused. Among all the faces that blurred past, one stopped him completely — a girl from Jhapa with a smile that felt like sunshine breaking through monsoon clouds. He didn't know it yet, but that single pause would change the entire direction of his life. Her name was Namita, and she was about to become his whole world.

💬
Chapter Two

Two Years of "Almost"

For two years, they danced around each other — talking, then not talking, then talking again. On and off, like the flickering lights of Kathmandu during load-shedding. Sometimes the universe has its own timeline, and theirs wasn't ready yet. But the thread between them never fully broke. No matter how many times the conversation paused, something always pulled them back. The universe was patient, even when they weren't.

🌙
Chapter Three

Sleepless Nights & Slow Falling

Somewhere between the on-and-offs, they started talking all night. Not because they had to — but because neither could say goodbye first. They shared everything — silly things, deep things, dreams, fears. She loved a movie called Devdas and told him — he said "me too!" and they both cried about it. She started reading books just to impress him, and he pretended not to notice — but his heart was already stolen. No plan, no strategy. Just two people falling without a parachute.

🏔️
Chapter Four

Seven Days Before the Sky

Then came the week that changed everything. Just seven days before she had to board a flight to Japan, Namita came to Kathmandu. They finally met — face to face, heart to heart. The boy who had only known her through a screen suddenly found her standing right in front of him, more real and more beautiful than any message could carry. His hands were shaking. He couldn't even speak at first. But they didn't need words — not yet.

🌄
Chapter Five

Nagarkot — Where Forever Started

They drove up to Nagarkot, where the mountains hold the sky. On their very first date — a night stay above the clouds — something shifted in the universe. They fit together like two opposite ends of a puzzle that had been searching for each other. Under those Himalayan stars, he kissed her for the first time. And they made a promise: to never give up on their relationship. That promise echoes in every breath he takes, still, to this day.

✈️
Chapter Six

Two Flights, Two Worlds

Seven days. That's all they had. But those seven days held a lifetime of memories. Then she flew east — to Japan. Months later, he flew west — to America. Omiya and Albany, separated by 10,800 kilometers and fourteen hours. The airport goodbye broke something inside him. But love doesn't care about distance. It just keeps showing up — in a good morning text that arrives at midnight, in a video call that starts with a fight and ends with "I love you."

💪
Chapter Seven

The Storms We Weathered

Let's be honest — it wasn't always beautiful. They fought. A lot. About time zones, about silences that stretched too long, about insecurities that crept in during lonely nights. She carried financial struggles and family pressure; he carried the weight of being lost — too introverted, too directionless, drinking too much beer. But here's what matters: they never gave up. She pushed him to go to the gym, to find a direction, to become better. And he stood beside her through every battle, every insecurity, every tear. They didn't just survive the storms — they grew through them.

🧩
Chapter Eight

Two Opposite Pieces, One Perfect Picture

He's the perfectionist, the overthinker, the one who plans five steps ahead. She's the warm one, the carefree spirit, the one who walks into a room and makes everyone feel like they belong. He's smart; she's wise. He's careful; she's brave. She taught him what real love feels like — not the movie kind, but the kind that stays through fights and distance and exhaustion. She was his first love. His first real relationship. His first experience of being chosen completely. Together, they don't just complete each other — they make each other better.

🌟
Chapter Nine

The Strongest Girl He Knows

She handles everything on her own — this girl from a simple family in Jhapa who dreamed of giving her parents a better life. The youngest child who carries more weight than anyone sees. She went to Japan alone, studied hard, passed her language school, graduated gaako, and is now pursuing kaigosenmon gakko. With her own hard-earned money, she sent her father an iPhone — not because he asked, but because his smile is her biggest reward. She never keeps anything in her heart. She is raw, real, genuine. She is the most authentic person he has ever met. And he loves every single part of her — especially the parts she thinks are flaws.

♾️
Chapter Ten

To Be Continued... with Gaurangi

Sometimes he closes his eyes and imagines a little house, filled with her laughter. And a tiny girl running through the rooms — their Gaurangi. She'll have her mother's eyes and her father's stubborn spirit. The pages ahead are blank and beautiful, waiting to be filled with reunions, new adventures, and one day — no more distance at all. This fairy tale doesn't end here, Mutu. Because the best stories never do. Everything he's doing — the PhD, the gym, the late nights — it's all for this future. For her. For them. For Gaurangi. 🌸
